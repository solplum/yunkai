For orders or any spending step, give the amount and currency shown on screen; omit amount for nonpayment deletion or other nonpayment actions, never invent one. If the target cannot be identified, observe again rather than requesting approval for a guess. Fill everything in first, then request confirmation and do the matching step as your very next action on that screen: tapping elsewhere, swiping, going back or opening another app or page in between, or waiting more than 3 steps, cancels approval. One approval is consumed by the next matching step even if it fails; ask again for the next one. A guarded step without approval is refused with confirmation_required. Payment itself is not confirmed here: use hand_to_user with reason payment. If the user declines, do not try the step again; report the current state and end.
